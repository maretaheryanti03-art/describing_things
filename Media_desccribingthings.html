📖 Teori Dasar: Describing Things

Mendeskripsikan benda (Describing Things) adalah keterampilan memberikan informasi rinci tentang suatu objek, termasuk ciri fisik, karakteristik, bahan, dan fungsinya. Kita menggunakan Adjectives (Kata Sifat) dan Nouns (Kata Benda).

1. Kategori Adjectives (Klik untuk buka) ▼

Ada banyak jenis kata sifat. Arahkan mouse (hover) pada kata untuk melihat artinya:

Opinion (Pendapat): Beautiful Amazing Boring

Size & Shape (Ukuran & Bentuk): Huge Round Slim

Material (Bahan): Wooden Metallic Cotton

2. Urutan Adjectives (OSASCOMP) ▼

Jika ada lebih dari satu adjective, kita harus mengikuti aturan OSASCOMP:

  1. Opinion (Beautiful)
  2. Size (Big)
  3. Age (Old)
  4. Shape (Round)
  5. Color (Red)
  6. Origin (Indonesian)
  7. Material (Wooden)
  8. Purpose (Sleeping - sleeping bag)

Contoh: A beautiful (O) small (S) old (A) wooden (M) box.

3. Sentence Patterns (Pola Kalimat) ▼

Pola 1: Subject + To Be + Adjective

The smartphone is expensive.

Pola 2: Subject + To Be + Adjective + Noun

It is an expensive smartphone.

Pola 3: Subject + Has/Have + Adjective + Noun

The car has a leather interior.

Part A: Multiple Choice (HOTS)

1. Rina wants to buy a bag made of animal skin. She goes to a leather shop. Which sentence best describes the material of the bag she wants?

2. Look at the adjectives: (1) Indonesian (2) Beautiful (3) Small (4) Wooden. What is the correct order to describe a "craft box"?

3. "The smartphone has a cracked screen and the battery drains quickly." What is the writer's purpose in writing the sentence?

4. Budi: "I lost my pen."
Andi: "Is it the ... pen I gave you last week?"
Choose the most appropriate adjective to fill in the blank.

5. Which of the following objects is best described by the adjectives: "Sharp, Stainless, Useful"?

6. "My grandfather has an antique watch. It is made of pure gold and it still works perfectly." What can we infer about the watch?

7. If you want to describe a "Sleeping Bag", what is the main function of the object based on its name?

8. "The room is very ... because it has no windows and the lights are off."

9. Analyze the sentence: "She wore a beautiful, long, red, silk dress." Which word indicates the MATERIAL of the dress?

10. Why is it important to use specific adjectives when describing a product for a catalog?

Part B: Essay

Jawablah pertanyaan berikut dengan bahasa Inggris yang baik dan benar!

11. Describe your favorite item in your bedroom (at least 3 sentences). Mention its color, size, and material!

12. Arrange these adjectives into a correct sentence describing a car: (Italian / red / fast / sports / a). Write the full sentence!

13. What is the difference between describing a person's physical appearance and describing a thing? Give one example for each!

14. Imagine you are selling a "Smartwatch". Write a short promotional paragraph (3-4 sentences) describing its features using at least 3 adjectives!

15. Explain why the order of adjectives (OSASCOMP) is important in English grammar. What happens if we say "a red big car" instead of "a big red car"?
